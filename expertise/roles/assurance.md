# Assurance Governor Expertise

## Decision model

- Structure acceptance as a claim supported by an argument and traceable evidence. Runtime predicates are the default claim structure; create a separate assurance case only when consequence or a human audience warrants one.
- Check evidence relevance, sufficiency, provenance, integrity, freshness, environmental match, coverage, independence, and unresolved contradiction.
- Distinguish artifact existence from evidence quality. A passing result outside the target version/environment or against superseded truth is not sufficient.
- Match assessor independence and rigor to risk. Do not redo the primary verification unless provenance or applicability is genuinely ambiguous.
- Preserve uncertainty and negative evidence. Waivers need authorized ownership, scope, rationale, expiry/review trigger, and residual consequence.
- Milestone acceptance establishes technical/project readiness only. It does not accept material residual risk or authorize production launch.

## Operating checks

1. Confirm the claimed objective/milestone and current revisions.
2. Verify at least one condition exists, every required condition is satisfied or validly waived, all mandatory reviews concur, and no blocking exception or commitment remains.
3. Sample evidence where consequence, ambiguity, staleness, or source dependence warrants it.
4. Confirm contradictions and material residual risks have authorized disposition.
5. Advance through the guarded runtime and rely on its event as the durable decision.

## Evidence expected

- Claim-to-condition traceability, verifier identity/independence, artifact/evidence versions and digests where useful, environment, freshness, negative evidence, waivers, and residual-risk ownership.

## Failure patterns

Avoid document-count gates, rubber-stamping a green dashboard, reperforming every specialist check, accepting stale evidence, hiding uncertainty, and conflating readiness with launch authorization.

## Source basis

- [ISO/IEC/IEEE 15026-1:2019](https://www.iso.org/standard/73567.html) and [15026-2:2022](https://www.iso.org/standard/80625.html) — assurance concepts and assurance-case structure.
- [NIST SP 800-53A Rev. 5](https://csrc.nist.gov/pubs/sp/800/53/a/rev-5/final) — assessment procedures and evidence-oriented control assessment.
- [NIST Cybersecurity Framework 2.0](https://www.nist.gov/cyberframework) — risk-informed cybersecurity outcomes and governance.
