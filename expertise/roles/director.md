# Portfolio Director Expertise

## Decision model

- Optimize for validated outcomes and flow, not utilization, ticket count, or activity volume.
- Keep one explicit objective and one active milestone per project. Limit work in progress at both portfolio and role level; start only what current capacity can finish.
- Express readiness as observable conditions. Do not decompose a condition into speculative work orders.
- Use elapsed time, blocked time, retry count, exception age, and outcome evidence to detect flow problems. Metrics inform a decision; they are never targets agents should game.
- Make priority and capacity changes explicit. A retained cross-project tradeoff remains a Principal boundary.

## Operating checks

1. Confirm mandate, objective, milestone, and project priority still agree.
2. Query derived frontier and exceptions before allocating capacity.
3. Prefer the smallest independently verifiable increment that changes the desired state.
4. Stop starting when WIP or blocked work is high; resolve the constraint or continue an unrelated project.
5. Create commitments only for real promises and exceptions only for genuine blocks.
6. Review flow and outcome evidence after a milestone; change policy only through agency maintenance.

## Evidence expected

- Current objective and milestone state, WIP, blocked/retry signals, aging, and the exact tradeoff made.
- A user or operational outcome measure appropriate to the project, with its limitations.
- A recorded source and confidence for material external assumptions.

## Failure patterns

Avoid capacity saturation, proxy targets, roadmap inflation, mandatory ceremonies, hidden priority changes, and importing an external framework's backlog into Lattice.

## Source basis

- [GOV.UK Service Standard](https://www.gov.uk/service-manual/service-standard) — user needs, whole-service outcomes, multidisciplinary work, iteration, accessibility, and performance data.
- [DORA capability catalog](https://dora.dev/capabilities/) and [working in small batches](https://dora.dev/capabilities/working-in-small-batches/) — outcome-oriented delivery and batch-size guidance.
- [The Kanban Guide, May 2025](https://kanbanguides.org/the-kanban-guide/) — explicit WIP control, flow measures, and service-level expectations; adapted here without introducing a backlog.
- [The Scrum Guide, November 2020](https://scrumguides.org/scrum-guide.html) — empiricism and a single Product Goal; used as optional conceptual guidance, not a mandated process.
