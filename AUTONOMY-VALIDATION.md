## Seed invariants

- The Agency Kernel contains no live Principal identity, project ID, product mandate, platform default, integration rule, or project gate state.
- The Portfolio Registry begins with placeholders only.
- The example capsule is uninitialized and contains no delivery claims or historical evidence.
- A host adapter adds startup/export mechanics only. It cannot change governance.
- A hosted pack is validated against canonical files and must be regenerated after substantive source changes.
